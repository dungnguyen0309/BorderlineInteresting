Time-zone areas for the "Time zones" map, built by scripts/build_timezones.py.

Source: timezone-boundary-builder release 2026f (timezones.geojson, one area per IANA zone),
https://github.com/evansiroky/timezone-boundary-builder
Data (c) OpenStreetMap contributors, https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright

These files are a derived database and are made available under the Open Database License 1.0:
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

zones-lo.json  simplified to 0.6% of vertices, for the whole-globe view
zones-hi.json  simplified to 6% of vertices, for zoomed-in views
Each zone keeps its tzid and a label point (lx, ly). Coordinates are quantized TopoJSON.
